# _nsigned - The DIY Music Magazine 

**_nsigned** is a music review site for small, DIY
artists. The concept is that musicians can post their music,
and other users can write up reviews that are published and
displayed like an editorial site.

The site was inspired by forums and music communities of the 2000s,
before they were made apparently obsolete by one-size-fits-all platforms such
as Reddit and Facebook. It hinges on the belief that there are people out there
who want to talk about and share all the weird music they find.
